Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan anytime? ↓
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and billing adjustments are made accordingly. You'll have access to new features right away.
What payment methods do you accept? ↓
We accept major credit cards, bank transfers, and popular Egyptian mobile payment solutions including Vodafone Cash and Orange Money for your convenience.
How much can I save with yearly billing? ↓
With yearly billing, you save 20% on all plans! Standard plan saves you EGP 480 per year, and Premium saves EGP 1,200 per year. That's like getting 2.4 months free!
Is there a long-term contract requirement? ↓
No long-term contracts required. All plans are flexible - monthly or yearly as you prefer. You can cancel anytime, and your current plan remains active until the end of your billing period.
How does the commission system work? ↓
Flowlyne takes a small commission only when you successfully complete projects through our platform. Basic plan: 10%, Standard: 8%, Premium: 5%. No upfront fees or hidden charges.
