Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan anytime?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and billing adjustments are made accordingly.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit cards, bank transfers, and mobile payment solutions popular in Egypt including Vodafone Cash and Orange Money.
Is there a contract or commitment period?
No long-term contracts required. All plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. Your current plan remains active until the end of your billing period.
What happens if I cancel my subscription?
You'll automatically be moved to the Basic (Free) plan. Your account and data remain intact, but premium features will no longer be accessible.
